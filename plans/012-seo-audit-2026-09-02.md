## B. Advisor review (sol)

**Recommendation:** prioritize recovering legacy traffic, making contact effortless, and publishing verified trust signals; defer schema enrichment and cosmetic metadata work. The accepted tradeoff is leaving some technical polish undone while the owner addresses the few items most likely to affect local visibility and inquiries.

### 1. Priority and severity corrections
- **H1 legacy 404s** and **H2 missing homepage phone/email** are correctly **High**. They lose existing equity and add friction on the principal landing page.
- Raise **M1 licensure visibility** to **High** once verified. On a YMYL solo-practitioner site, a visible Arizona license and authoritative board/profile links matter more than most metadata.
- Lower **H3 title/description length** to **Medium**. Google has pixel-based, query-dependent rewriting—not hard 60/155-character limits—and descriptions are mainly a CTR lever, not a direct ranking factor.
- Lower **H4 performance** to **Medium pending PageSpeed/CrUX evidence**. The opaque `#page-transition` and synchronous Tailwind CDN are credible UX risks, but the audit did not measure LCP or conversion impact; precompilation is already explicitly deferred in `plans/README.md`.
- Lower **M4 schema thinness** to **Low**. `ContactPage`, `Offer`, `paymentAccepted`, and richer `areaServed` markup are unlikely to move rankings. Keep **M5 NAP/hours inconsistency** at Medium, potentially High if the GBP itself is wrong.

### 2. Wrong, risky, or counterproductive fixes
- Do **not** add `medicalSpecialty: "Psychiatric"` in `index.html`. Psychology is not psychiatry; this is an inaccurate clinical classification.
- Do not add `knowsLanguage`, a license number, board affiliation, Yelp URL, or “Licensed psychologist” copy until the owner verifies each fact. The proposed description—`"Licensed psychologist Dr. Marie Haddox… Telehealth across Arizona"`—contains regulated/licensure and service-area claims that need confirmation.
- Do not keyword-expand the visible `.faq-btn` labels into long strings such as `"Does Spectrum Counseling in Gilbert, AZ take insurance?"`; that hurts the calm tone and risks cramped mobile buttons. Because FAQ rich results are improbable here, remove the `FAQPage` block or make it faithfully mirror the concise visible content.
- Cloudflare 301s are the right redirect mechanism, but do not map every historical page by filename alone. In particular, `/docs.htm` → `/new-client-form/` sends equity to a `noindex` page, and `/therapists.htm` → `/#specializations` may be topically wrong. Inspect archived content and Search Console/backlinks first.
- `priceRange: "$$"` is conventional; replacing it with `"$220 per session"` and adding two duplicate Offers is schema churn, not a material SEO fix.

### 3. Important omissions
- The audit underweights **Google Business Profile operations**: correct primary category, services, appointment URL, hours, photos, website URL, and ongoing review handling are central to local-pack visibility.
- It misses the potential value of **substantive service pages**. Redirecting anxiety, depression, couples, gender, and sexuality URLs to one JavaScript slider preserves little query-specific relevance; Search Console data should identify one or two pages worth rebuilding.
- `index.html` republishes patient-like testimonials (“J.L.” and “B.F.”). For a therapy practice, confirm consent and professional-ethics compliance; linking to third-party reviews is safer than republishing sensitive endorsements.
